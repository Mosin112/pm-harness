# Jobs to be done (JTBD) cheat sheet

A job is the progress a person is trying to make in a particular situation. Products are
"hired" to do jobs and "fired" when something does the job better.

## Job statement

```
When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome].
```

- **Situation**: the trigger moment, specific enough to picture. Not a persona.
- **Motivation**: what they are trying to get done, in their words, solution-free.
- **Outcome**: how they will know it worked.

Good: *When I'm waiting for a better price to buy, I want to be told the moment it gets there,
so I can act without watching the chart all day.*

Weak: *As a user, I want price alerts.* (names a solution, no situation, no outcome)

## Forces of progress

| Force | Question |
|---|---|
| **Push** of the situation | What is painful or frustrating about today? |
| **Pull** of the new solution | What does the better future look like? |
| **Anxiety** of the new | What worries them about switching? |
| **Habit** of the present | What do they do today that is "good enough"? |

A new product wins when push + pull outweigh anxiety + habit. Design to reduce anxiety and
habit as much as to increase pull.

## Competing solutions

List what people hire today for this job, including non-obvious ones: a spreadsheet, a friend,
a third-party app, doing nothing. That list is the real competition.

## Outcome measures

Turn the expected outcome into one primary success metric and one or two guardrails
(things that must not get worse).
